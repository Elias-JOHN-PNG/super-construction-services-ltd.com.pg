About Us

Super Construction Services Ltd is a Papua New Guinea–owned construction company delivering quality building, civil, and infrastructure projects.

Areas Of Specializations

Residential construction, civil works, road construction, bridge construction, renovation, and project supervision.

Years of Experience

Over 10 years of construction and infrastructure experience.

Managing Director – Mr. Willie Dus

Mr. Willie Dus, from Yaken Lower Mendi, Southern Highlands Province, holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Environmental Health from the University of Papua New Guinea.

He worked with OTML and later served as General Manager at Cane Construction Company before establishing Super Construction Services Ltd.

Locations

Nipa, Port Moresby, Lae, and Kiribati.
